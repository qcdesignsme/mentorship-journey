When a relationship goes quiet

Ghosting branches off during engagement, and the two sides experience it very differently. This is the finding that led to two extra journeys.

MentorGets ghosted

  1. Mentee stops replyingMy mentee isn't responding. What did I do wrong?

    Few resources for handling an unresponsive mentee

  2. Asks for helpI think I need help getting in touch with my mentee.

    Replies are mostly automated, which can feel impersonal

  3. Decides what's nextShould I get another mentee? Do I want to do this again?

What would help: Guidance for an unresponsive mentee, encouraging nudges so mentors don't blame themselves, and a community of mentors who've been there.

MenteePulls away

  1. Stops engagingThis mentorship isn't serving my needs.

    Not enough structure to keep it going

  2. Messages keep comingWhy am I still getting so many messages? I thought I unsubscribed.

    Message campaigns aren't connected, so unsubscribing from one doesn't stop the rest

What would help: Nudges toward a respectful goodbye so the mentor isn't left hanging, and messages that stop after “STOP.”

What the research showed

Key insight

Expectations shape both how people communicate and how engaged their counterpart stays.

Top two themes for mentors and mentees: program expectations, and engagement of the other person.

What we set out to solve

How can we align participant expectations with program expectations?
How can we give participants agency over their experience?
How can we increase engagement through resources relevant to participants' goals?
